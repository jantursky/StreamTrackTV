## Which TVs work?

Android TV and Google TV devices with Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer. Fire TV runs a fork of Android and is not tested.

## Is there a hosted version?

No. The public demo uses seeded data. Real data lives only in your own Vercel and Supabase accounts.

## Does it see what I watch?

No. It sees which app is in the foreground and for how long. It cannot see titles, channels or anything on the screen.

## Why an accessibility service?

Android gives apps no other way to count remote key presses. The service is limited to key events. See the [TV app guide](/guides/tv-app).

## Why is it not on Google Play?

Play puts strict rules on accessibility apps. The APK is signed in CI and published on GitHub Releases as `StreamTrackTV-<version>.apk`.

## What does it cost?

The software is free and open source. Vercel and Supabase free tiers fit a household. Check their current limits.

## Can several TVs report to one account?

Yes. Pair each TV separately. Devices can be grouped under household profiles.

## What happens when the TV is offline?

Hourly buckets stay on the TV and go out with the next successful sync. Sending them twice is harmless.

## Can I move my data elsewhere?

Export CSV or JSON from the settings page, or query your Supabase database directly.
